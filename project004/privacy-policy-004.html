Privacy Policy

Ruler — Android app, package com.ub.ruler
Last updated: 2026-08-23

The short version. Ruler asks for a single permission — internet access — and uses it for exactly one thing: sending an anonymous crash report through Firebase Crashlytics if the app fails, so we can find and fix the problem. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything else the app writes — your saved measurements, your settings, your calibration — stays in the app's private storage on your device and is never transmitted.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by Ufuk Mobil Uygulama Atölyesi ("we", "us"), the data controller for the processing described here.

Privacy contact: ufukmobiluygulamaatolyesi@gmail.com. We answer privacy questions and requests sent to this address.

This policy is published in English and Turkish. Where the two differ, the English text governs.

2. What we do not collect

INTERNET is the only permission the app requests — open the permission list before you install and you will see nothing else there. Beyond the crash reports described in section 4:

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No advertising and no analytics. The app contains no AdMob, Firebase Analytics or similar SDK — Crashlytics is the only Firebase component in the app, and it exists solely for crash reports.
  • No camera, no location, no contacts, no microphone, no files outside the app's own storage.
  • Your saved measurements, settings and calibration are never transmitted — see section 3.

3. What is stored on your device

Not collecting data is not the same as not storing it. The app writes to its own private storage, sandboxed by Android and inaccessible to other apps:

WhatWhereContents
Saved measurementsLocal database (Room)Measurement geometry, unit, calibration factor, date, the name you gave it
PreferencesLocal storage (DataStore)Unit, mode, calibration factor, theme, colours
Review prompt countersLocal storage (DataStore)How many measurements you have saved, how many distinct days you opened the app, when you were last asked to rate it

None of this is personal data in any meaningful sense, and none of it ever leaves your device through the app. You can review and delete your saved measurements from inside the app at any time, and exporting them as CSV is something you do, through Android's own share sheet — the app itself never transmits the file.

4. Crash reports

If the app fails, it can send a diagnostic report through Google Firebase Crashlytics. This is the one thing that leaves your device, and the only reason the app holds the INTERNET permission at all.

DataWhy
Crash logs and stack tracesIdentify the fault
Device model, Android version, app versionReproduce the fault
Firebase Installation ID (a pseudonymous identifier)Group multiple reports from the same installation

There is no toggle for this. Unlike an ad SDK, a crash reporter that only some installations report from is a lot less useful for finding what is actually broken, so it stays on for everyone. It is also the only network activity in the app: no report is sent unless the app crashes.

Google acts as our service provider for this data under its own terms; it is not sold or shared with advertisers, and Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Android's own backup

The app allows Android's built-in backup (allowBackup), so if you have backup enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your own Google account. This is a platform feature, not something we do or have access to — we operate no servers and receive nothing from it. You can turn device backup off in your phone's own settings if you would rather it not happen.

6. Google Play's rating prompt

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it, using Google's official Play In-App Review component. That prompt and the rating flow are handled entirely by the Play Store app already installed on your device; the review library talks to it directly and does not make its own network requests or collect anything on our behalf.

7. Children

This app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country); a crash report contains no information that identifies who was using the app. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

8. Legal bases (GDPR) and your rights

For the crash data described in section 4, we rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — diagnosing and fixing faults in software we publish is a narrow, expected purpose that does not need your separate consent, and it stops the moment the report is sent; we hold no ongoing profile of you.

The measurements and settings described in section 3 never reach us at all, so there is no legal basis to state for them — they exist solely on your device, under your sole control. You already hold and control that data yourself, directly in the app (delete a saved measurement, reset a setting) or through Android's own Settings → Apps → Ruler → Clear storage, which removes all of it at once. Uninstalling the app does the same.

For the crash data itself, you may have the right to access, delete or object to its processing; because the app has no account system, a request needs the Firebase Installation ID concerned to be actionable, which we cannot look up on your behalf. Write to the address in section 1 and we will act on what we can. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority at any time. In Türkiye, that is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

9. Residents of California and other US states

We do not sell personal information, and the crash data described in section 4 is processed by Google solely as our service provider, on our behalf and under contract — not for Google's own purposes. Nothing described in this policy qualifies as a "sale" or as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under the CCPA/CPRA or similar state laws.

10. Retention

  • On your device: your measurements and settings remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's own retention schedule; we hold no independent copy.

11. Security

The data described in section 3 lives in storage that Android isolates to this app alone; no other app can read it without your device being compromised. The one thing that does leave the device — a crash report — is sent over TLS.

12. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or stores ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Any such change would also be reflected in the app's Data safety section on Google Play before it ships.

Uygulama çökerse Google Firebase Crashlytics üzerinden bir tanılama raporu gönderebilir. Cihazınızdan çıkan tek şey budur ve uygulamanın INTERNET iznine sahip olmasının tek sebebi de bu.

Bunun için bir kapatma anahtarı yok. Google, bu veri bakımından kendi koşulları altında hizmet sağlayıcımız olarak davranır; veri reklamverenlere satılmaz veya paylaşılmaz. Google'ın bu verileri işleyişi Google Gizlilik Politikası'na tabidir.

Bu, bizim yaptığımız veya erişebildiğimiz bir şey değil, platformun bir özelliğidir — sunucu işletmiyoruz ve bundan hiçbir şey almıyoruz.
