Privacy Policy

Ruler — Android app, package com.ub.ruler
Last updated: 2026-10-14

The short version. Measuring is ad-free and offline. Data leaves your device in only two cases: an anonymous crash report through Firebase Crashlytics if the app fails, and — only if you choose to watch one — a rewarded video ad from Google AdMob that unlocks Pro features for one hour. There is no account and no analytics. Everything else the app writes — your saved measurements, your settings, your calibration — stays in the app's private storage on your device and is never transmitted.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by Ufuk Mobil Uygulama Atölyesi ("we", "us"), the data controller for the processing described here.

Privacy contact: ufukmobiluygulamaatolyesi@gmail.com. We answer privacy questions and requests sent to this address.

This policy is published in English and Turkish. Where the two differ, the English text governs.

2. What we do not collect

Beyond the crash reports described in section 4 and the optional rewarded ads described in section 5:

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No analytics. The app contains no Firebase Analytics or similar SDK. Crashlytics is the only Firebase component, and it exists solely for crash reports.
  • No ads while you measure. There are no banner or interstitial ads, and the ad SDK is not started when the app opens — only when you ask for a rewarded ad (section 5).
  • No camera, no location, no contacts, no microphone, no files outside the app's own storage.
  • Your saved measurements, settings and calibration are never transmitted — see section 3.

3. What is stored on your device

Not collecting data is not the same as not storing it. The app writes to its own private storage, sandboxed by Android and inaccessible to other apps:

WhatWhereContents
Saved measurementsLocal database (Room)Measurement geometry, unit, calibration factor, date, the name you gave it
PreferencesLocal storage (DataStore)Unit, mode, calibration factor, theme, colours
Review prompt countersLocal storage (DataStore)How many measurements you have saved, how many distinct days you opened the app, when you were last asked to rate it

None of this is personal data in any meaningful sense, and none of it ever leaves your device through the app. You can review and delete your saved measurements from inside the app at any time, and exporting them as CSV is something you do, through Android's own share sheet — the app itself never transmits the file.

4. Crash reports

If the app fails, it can send a diagnostic report through Google Firebase Crashlytics. Apart from a rewarded ad you choose to watch (section 5), this is the only thing that leaves your device.

DataWhy
Crash logs and stack tracesIdentify the fault
Device model, Android version, app versionReproduce the fault
Firebase Installation ID (a pseudonymous identifier)Group multiple reports from the same installation

There is no toggle for this. Unlike an ad SDK, a crash reporter that only some installations report from is a lot less useful for finding what is actually broken, so it stays on for everyone. No report is sent unless the app crashes.

Google acts as our service provider for this data under its own terms; it is not sold or shared with advertisers, and Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Some features are part of Pro. Instead of buying Pro, you can unlock those features for one hour by watching a short video ad, served by Google AdMob. This is entirely optional: ads appear only when you tap the unlock button, and the ad SDK is not started before that. If you never tap it, the app makes no ad requests at all.

When you do request an ad, AdMob may collect and process:

Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Deliver an ad that works on your device

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. In these regions the app first shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can accept, decline, or choose individual purposes. If you decline, you may still be shown non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options in the app.

Everywhere. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's own settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices).

Google processes ad data under its own terms and is responsible for its own use of it. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive your advertising ID or any of the data above; we only see aggregate reports (such as how many ads were shown).

6. Pro purchase (Google Play Billing)

Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details and the purchase record stay with Google Play; the app never sees or stores your card or account details. It only asks Google Play whether Pro is active on this device.

7. Android's own backup

The app allows Android's built-in backup (allowBackup), so if you have backup enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your own Google account. This is a platform feature, not something we do or have access to — we operate no servers and receive nothing from it. You can turn device backup off in your phone's own settings if you would rather it not happen.

8. Google Play's rating prompt

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it, using Google's official Play In-App Review component. That prompt and the rating flow are handled entirely by the Play Store app already installed on your device; the review library talks to it directly and does not make its own network requests or collect anything on our behalf.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country); a crash report contains no information that identifies who was using the app, and rewarded ads are shown only when requested. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

10. Legal bases (GDPR) and your rights

For the crash data described in section 4, we rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — diagnosing and fixing faults in software we publish is a narrow, expected purpose that does not need your separate consent, and it stops the moment the report is sent; we hold no ongoing profile of you.

For the rewarded ads described in section 5, storing and accessing information on your device and personalised advertising rely on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected through Google's consent form. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options; withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

The measurements and settings described in section 3 never reach us at all, so there is no legal basis to state for them — they exist solely on your device, under your sole control. You already hold and control that data yourself, directly in the app (delete a saved measurement, reset a setting) or through Android's own Settings → Apps → Ruler → Clear storage, which removes all of it at once. Uninstalling the app does the same.

For the crash data itself, you may have the right to access, delete or object to its processing; because the app has no account system, a request needs the Firebase Installation ID concerned to be actionable, which we cannot look up on your behalf. Write to the address in section 1 and we will act on what we can. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority at any time. In Türkiye, that is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

11. Residents of California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. The crash data described in section 4 is processed by Google solely as our service provider, on our behalf and under contract — not for Google's own purposes.

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad (section 5), the identifiers and device data listed there are disclosed to Google, which may use them for advertising. Under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws this may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out by not requesting rewarded ads, or by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ads personalisation in your phone's settings.

12. Retention

  • On your device: your measurements and settings remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's own retention schedule; we hold no independent copy.
  • Ad data: retained by Google according to its own policies; we hold no copy.

13. Security

The data described in section 3 lives in storage that Android isolates to this app alone; no other app can read it without your device being compromised. What does leave the device — a crash report, or an ad request you made — is sent over TLS.

14. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or stores ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Any such change would also be reflected in the app's Data safety section on Google Play before it ships.

Android'in kendi cihaz yedekleme özelliği (bkz. 7. bölüm) ayrıca sizin kendi Google hesabınıza yönelik bir platform işlevidir; bizim tarafımızdan yapılan veya erişilen bir aktarım değildir.

İlgili kişinin hakları (KVKK m. 11)

Kanunun 11. maddesi uyarınca veri sorumlusuna başvurarak şunları talep edebilirsiniz:

  • Kişisel verinizin işlenip işlenmediğini öğrenme,
  • İşlenmişse buna ilişkin bilgi talep etme,
  • İşlenme amacını ve amacına uygun kullanılıp kullanılmadığını öğrenme,
  • Yurt içinde veya yurt dışında aktarıldığı üçüncü kişileri bilme,
  • Eksik veya yanlış işlenmişse düzeltilmesini isteme,
  • Kanunun 7. maddesindeki şartlar çerçevesinde silinmesini veya yok edilmesini isteme,
  • Düzeltme, silme ve yok etme işlemlerinin aktarıldığı üçüncü kişilere bildirilmesini isteme,
  • Münhasıran otomatik sistemlerle analiz edilmesi suretiyle aleyhinize bir sonuç ortaya çıkmasına itiraz etme,
  • Kanuna aykırı işleme sebebiyle zarara uğramanız hâlinde zararın giderilmesini talep etme.

Uygulamada hesap bulunmadığından sizi adınızla eşleştiremeyiz; çökme verisine ilişkin taleplerde ilgili Firebase kurulum kimliğinin bildirilmesi gerekir. Reklam verisi bize ulaşmadığından, bu veriye ilişkin talepler için Google'ın kendi araçlarını ve telefonunuzun reklam ayarlarını kullanabilirsiniz (bkz. 5. bölüm). Talepleriniz ufukmobiluygulamaatolyesi@gmail.com adresine iletilebilir; başvurular en geç otuz gün içinde sonuçlandırılır. Başvurunuzun reddedilmesi, verilen cevabı yetersiz bulmanız veya süresinde cevap verilmemesi hâlinde Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu'na şikâyette bulunabilirsiniz.
