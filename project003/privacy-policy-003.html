Privacy Policy

Super Flashlight — Android app, package com.ufukbagci.superflashlight
Last updated: 2026-08-02

The short version. Super Flashlight does not ask for camera or location permission, has no account or login, and never uploads what you type into Morse mode. Three things do leave your device: optional crash reports, data used to serve ads, and your purchase status if you buy Pro. You can switch crash reports off in Settings, and buying Pro turns the advertising component off entirely — it is never even started.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by Ufuk Mobil Uygulama Atölyesi ("we", "us"), the data controller for the processing described here.

Privacy contact: ufukmobiluygulamaatolyesi@gmail.com. We answer privacy questions and requests sent to this address.

This policy is published in English and Turkish. Where the two differ, the English text governs.

2. What we do not collect

Stated first, because it is the larger part of the answer:

  • No camera access. The flashlight uses Android's torch API (CameraManager.setTorchMode), so the app does not request the CAMERA permission and cannot see through your camera.
  • No location permission. The app never requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and calls no location API. (An approximate, IP-derived location is nonetheless processed by the advertising component — see section 4.)
  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • Morse text stays on the device. Whatever you type into Morse mode is turned into light locally and is never transmitted or stored off-device.
  • Your settings stay on the device. Preferences are written to encrypted local storage and are never uploaded.
  • No contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, call logs, SMS or browsing history.

3. Crash reports (optional)

To find and fix crashes, the app can send diagnostic reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics.

DataWhy
Crash logs and stack tracesIdentify the fault
Device model, Android version, app version, session stateReproduce the fault
Firebase Installation ID (a pseudonymous identifier)Group multiple reports from the same installation

You can turn this off: Settings → Privacy → Crash reports. Turning it off stops all collection from that point on. Google acts as our service provider for this data; it is not shared with advertisers.

4. Advertising

The free version of the app shows a single banner at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). The SDK collects and shares the following with Google and its advertising partners:

Data typePurpose
Advertising ID, App set ID, account identifiersAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactions (launches, taps, ad views)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics and performance informationAnalytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location, estimated from your IP addressAdvertising, analytics

The approximate location above is worth spelling out: the app holds no location permission and reads no location sensor. Your IP address is nonetheless enough for the advertising system to estimate the general area you are in, and we disclose that because it happens.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. A list of the advertising partners that may receive it is published at Google's ad technology providers.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before any personalised advertising request is made. Your choice is stored on your device and honoured on later launches. You can change it at any time from [PRIVACY OPTIONS PATH].

How to limit or stop advertising

  • Buy Pro. If Pro is active, the advertising SDK is never initialised at all: no consent form, no ad request, no identifier is read.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads.
  • Withdraw consent (EEA/UK/CH) through the privacy options entry point described above.

5. Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are entered in Google's own purchase dialog and never reach the app; we do not see or store your card, address or billing information. The only thing the app keeps is a single flag on your device recording whether Pro is active, which it re-checks with Google Play on each launch so that refunds and account changes are reflected.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

7. Legal bases (GDPR)

If the GDPR applies to you, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — personalised advertising in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, given through the consent form described in section 4.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — crash diagnostics (the switch in Settings is how you object to it), fraud prevention and the security of the advertising system, and non-personalised advertising where you declined personalisation.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — completing and validating a Pro purchase.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to the address in section 1 and we will act on your request.

Because the app has no account system, we cannot look you up by name. To act on a request about advertising or crash data we may need the identifier concerned — for advertising, that is your advertising ID, which you can read in the Android settings linked above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Türkiye, that is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

9. Residents of California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. However, the sharing of advertising identifiers and app interaction data for personalised advertising described in section 4 may qualify as a "sale" or as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws. You can opt out by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in Android settings, or by buying Pro, which removes the advertising component entirely.

10. Retention

  • On your device: settings and the Pro flag remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's retention schedule.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we hold no independent copy of it.

11. Security

All data leaving the device is sent over TLS. Settings stored on the device are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. No data is stored on servers we operate — we run none.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes to advertising or data collection will also be reflected in the app's Data safety section on Google Play.
