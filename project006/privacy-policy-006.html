Privacy Policy

Bubble Level — Android app, package com.ub.bubblelevel
Last updated: 2026-10-07

The short version. Bubble Level has no account and no ads, and it never uses the advertising ID. Two things leave your device: anonymous usage statistics (which features are used — never the angles you measure or the names you give them) and crash reports, both through Google Firebase. Your saved measurements, settings and calibration stay in the app's private storage on your device and are never transmitted by the app.

Deleting your data

Bubble Level (Ufuk Mobil Uygulama Atölyesi) has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to or close. To delete your data:

  1. Data on your device (saved measurements, settings, calibration): delete measurements in the app, or open Settings → Apps → Bubble Level → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes it immediately.
  2. Usage statistics and crash reports held in Firebase: email ufukmobiluygulamaatolyesi@gmail.com with the subject "Bubble Level data deletion", your app version and the approximate date you used the app. We delete what we can identify within 30 days. Otherwise this data is deleted automatically: crash reports after 90 days, usage statistics after at most 14 months.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by Ufuk Mobil Uygulama Atölyesi ("we", "us"), the data controller for the processing described here.

Privacy contact: ufukmobiluygulamaatolyesi@gmail.com. We answer privacy questions and requests sent to this address.

This policy is published in English and Turkish. Where the two differ, the English text governs.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID. The app contains no ad SDK. The advertising-ID permissions that Firebase would normally add are removed from the app, advertising-ID collection is switched off and ad-personalisation signals are disabled.
  • No camera, no location, no contacts, no microphone, no files outside the app's own storage. The app reads the device's motion sensors (accelerometer / gravity) to show the level; those readings are processed on the device and are never sent anywhere.
  • Your saved measurements, the names you give them, your settings and your calibration are never transmitted — see section 3.

3. What is stored on your device

Not collecting data is not the same as not storing it. The app writes to its own private storage, sandboxed by Android and inaccessible to other apps:

WhatWhereContents
Saved measurementsLocal database (Room)Tool and mode, reference edge, angles, unit, whether it was a relative reading, date, the name you gave it
PreferencesLocal storage (DataStore)Unit, tolerance, decimals, theme and colours, sound / vibration / screen-on options, language
CalibrationLocal storage (DataStore)Sensor offsets measured during calibration, calibration profile
Review prompt countersLocal storage (DataStore)How many measurements you have saved, how many distinct days you opened the app, when you were last asked to rate it

None of this ever leaves your device through the app. You can review and delete your saved measurements from inside the app at any time. Sharing a measurement or exporting them as CSV is something you do, through Android's own share sheet, to an app you choose — the app itself never transmits them.

4. Usage statistics

To learn which features are actually used, the app sends a small, fixed set of events to Google Firebase Analytics:

EventWhat it carries
A screen was openedThe screen's name (for example "settings")
A measurement was savedMode and unit — not the measured value or its name
A calibration was savedWhich edge and which calibration profile
The Pro screen was shown / a purchase was startedWhich Pro feature it was about

Firebase associates these events with a pseudonymous app-instance identifier and adds basic technical information it collects automatically (device model, Android version, app version, country derived from the network, and session timing). The data is used only in aggregate, to decide what to improve. It is not used for advertising, not combined with an advertising ID and not used to build a profile of you.

5. Crash reports

If the app fails, it can send a diagnostic report through Google Firebase Crashlytics.

DataWhy
Crash logs and stack tracesIdentify the fault
Device model, Android version, app versionReproduce the fault
Firebase Installation ID (a pseudonymous identifier)Group multiple reports from the same installation

Google acts as our service provider for the data in sections 4 and 5 under the Firebase terms; it is not sold or shared with advertisers, and Google's handling of it is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy. There is currently no in-app switch for statistics or crash reports; uninstalling the app stops both immediately.

6. Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under its own terms; the app never sees or stores your name, card or other payment details. It only asks Google Play whether Pro has been purchased on your account, so that the Pro features can be unlocked.

7. Android's own backup

The app allows Android's built-in backup (allowBackup), so if you have backup enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your own Google account. This is a platform feature, not something we do or have access to — we operate no servers and receive nothing from it. You can turn device backup off in your phone's own settings if you would rather it not happen.

8. Google Play's rating prompt

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it, using Google's official Play In-App Review component. That prompt and the rating flow are handled entirely by the Play Store app already installed on your device; we receive nothing from it beyond the public rating and review you choose to post.

9. Permissions

  • VIBRATE — the optional vibration when the device is level.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — sending the statistics and crash reports in sections 4 and 5, and talking to Google Play for purchases.
  • BILLING — Google Play purchases (section 6).

The app does not request camera, location, contacts, microphone or storage permissions.

10. Children

This app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country); the statistics and crash reports contain no information that identifies who was using the app. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

11. Legal bases (GDPR) and your rights

For the usage statistics and crash data in sections 4 and 5, we rely on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): understanding which features are used and diagnosing faults in software we publish are narrow, expected purposes, and the data is pseudonymous and never used for advertising. For purchases (section 6), the basis is the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

The measurements, settings and calibration described in section 3 never reach us at all — they exist solely on your device, under your control. You can delete them in the app or through Android's Settings → Apps → Bubble Level → Clear storage, which removes all of it at once. Uninstalling the app does the same.

For the statistics and crash data, you may have the right to access, delete or object to its processing. Because the app has no account system, we cannot link this data to your name; write to the address in section 1 with your app version and the approximate date, and we will act on what we can. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority at any time. In Türkiye, that is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

12. Residents of California and other US states

We do not sell personal information, and the data described in sections 4 and 5 is processed by Google as our service provider, on our behalf and under contract. Nothing described in this policy qualifies as a "sale" or as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under the CCPA/CPRA or similar state laws.

13. Retention

  • On your device: your measurements, settings and calibration remain until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Usage statistics: kept by Firebase Analytics for at most 14 months, after which event-level data is deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's own retention schedule (currently 90 days); we hold no independent copy.

14. Security

The data described in section 3 lives in storage that Android isolates to this app alone; no other app can read it without your device being compromised. Everything the app sends — statistics, crash reports and purchase checks — travels over TLS.

15. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or stores ever changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Any such change would also be reflected in the app's Data safety section on Google Play before it ships.
