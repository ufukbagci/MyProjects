Privacy Policy

Simple FlashLight — Android app, package com.ufukbagci.myflashlight
Last updated: 2026-08-26

The short version. Simple FlashLight does not ask for camera or location permission, has no account or login, and everything you set — the light color, the click sound, the SOS pattern — stays on your device. The only thing that leaves your device is data used to serve the banner ad. Buying the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade turns the advertising component off entirely — it is never even started.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by Ufuk Mobil Uygulama Atölyesi ("we", "us"), the data controller for the processing described here.

Privacy contact: ufukmobiluygulamaatolyesi@gmail.com. We answer privacy questions and requests sent to this address.

This policy is published in English and Turkish. Where the two differ, the English text governs.

2. What we do not collect

Stated first, because it is the larger part of the answer:

  • No camera access. The flashlight uses Android's torch API (CameraManager.setTorchMode), so the app does not request the CAMERA permission and cannot see through your camera. The declared android.hardware.camera.flash feature is only used to tell Android the device needs a flash unit; it is not a permission and grants no access.
  • No location permission. The app never requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and calls no location API. (An approximate, IP-derived location is nonetheless processed by the advertising component — see section 3.)
  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • Your settings stay on the device. The selected light color, the click-sound toggle and the SOS state are written to local app storage and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • No contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, call logs, SMS or browsing history.

Notification and home screen widget

When you keep the torch on, the app runs a foreground service so Android does not turn the light off in the background; this shows a persistent notification while the torch is on, which is why the app requests the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission on Android 13 and newer. The optional home screen widget toggles the torch the same way the in-app button does. Neither the notification nor the widget sends any data anywhere — both act only on your device.

3. Advertising

The free version of the app shows a single banner at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). The SDK collects and shares the following with Google and its advertising partners:

Data typePurpose
Advertising ID, App set ID, account identifiersAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactions (launches, taps, ad views)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics and performance informationAnalytics, fraud prevention
Approximate location, estimated from your IP addressAdvertising, analytics

The approximate location above is worth spelling out: the app holds no location permission and reads no location sensor. Your IP address is nonetheless enough for the advertising system to estimate the general area you are in, and we disclose that because it happens.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. A list of the advertising partners that may receive it is published at Google's ad technology providers.

How to limit or stop advertising

  • Buy "Remove Ads". Once active, the advertising SDK is never initialised at all: no consent prompt, no ad request, no identifier is read.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads.
  • Withdraw consent (EEA/UK/CH) through the consent options the advertising SDK presents on first launch in those regions.

4. Removing ads

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are entered in Google's own purchase dialog and never reach the app; we do not see or store your card, address or billing information. The only thing the app keeps is a single flag on your device recording whether the purchase is active, which it re-checks with Google Play so that refunds and account changes are reflected.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

If the GDPR applies to you, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — personalised advertising in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, given through the consent options described in section 3.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — fraud prevention and the security of the advertising system, and non-personalised advertising where you declined personalisation.
  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — completing and validating the "Remove Ads" purchase.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to the address in section 1 and we will act on your request.

Because the app has no account system, we cannot look you up by name. To act on a request about advertising data we may need the identifier concerned — that is your advertising ID, which you can read in the Android settings linked above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Residents of California and other US states

We do not sell personal information for money. However, the sharing of advertising identifiers and app interaction data for personalised advertising described in section 3 may qualify as a "sale" or as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under the CCPA/CPRA and similar state laws. You can opt out by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in Android settings, or by buying "Remove Ads", which removes the advertising component entirely.

9. Retention

  • On your device: settings and the "Remove Ads" flag remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we hold no independent copy of it.

10. Security

All data leaving the device is sent over TLS. No data is stored on servers we operate — we run none.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes to advertising or data collection will also be reflected in the app's Data safety section on Google Play.
